# CalcWise Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 8, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 8, 2026

CalcWise is developed and published by KAVASRI Technologies.

KAVASRI Technologies respects user privacy and has designed CalcWise to operate with privacy as a core principle.

## Information Collection

CalcWise does not intentionally collect personal information through the application's core calculator functionality.

## Calculations

Calculations are processed locally on the user's device.

Calculation inputs and results are not transmitted to KAVASRI Technologies as part of the calculator's core operation.

## Calculation History

Calculation history, memory data, application preferences, and related settings are stored locally where applicable.

## Accounts

CalcWise does not require users to create an account to use its calculator functionality.

## Advertising

CalcWise does not contain advertising.

## Data Selling

KAVASRI Technologies does not sell CalcWise user calculation data.

## Internet Connectivity

CalcWise's calculator functionality is designed to operate offline.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken during development and distribution to protect the integrity of official CalcWise releases.

Users should obtain CalcWise through official distribution channels.

## Children's Privacy

CalcWise is a general-purpose calculator application and is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CalcWise, applicable requirements, or its distribution practices change.

The latest version will be published through official CalcWise channels.
